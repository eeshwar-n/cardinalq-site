What Cardinal is

And what it isn't.

Most confusion about the firm comes from assuming it's one of the things on the right.

Cardinal is

  • A proprietary trading firm. The capital is ours.
  • A research house that trades. The trading is how we find out whether we were right.
  • Small by design. One office, one culture, one log.
  • Permanent. Built to outlast everyone who started it.

Cardinal is not

  • A hedge fund. No outside capital, ever.
  • A signal service, a newsletter, or an education business.
  • A high-frequency shop. We win on understanding, not latency.
  • A prop shop. We don't sell evaluations, seats, or funding.
